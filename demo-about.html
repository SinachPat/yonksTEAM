Our Story

We got out. We can show you how.

Jason & Tyler Younker. A married duo who help successful but burned-out professionals get their lives back.

📸 Jason & Tyler — on the road

The Full Story

Chapter 1

The Burnout

Jason in IT, burning out in 2017. Driving Uber to buy back freedom. Tyler in sales, hitting the impossible number, getting a congratulations email and a nice lunch. Two different careers, one identical feeling: this can't be all there is.

Chapter 2

The Escape

Eight years ago, they traded careers for income-producing assets. Full-time travelers ever since. A five-year-old who thinks it's normal to ask which city she'll live in tomorrow. They didn't retire — they redesigned the relationship between work and life.

Chapter 3

The Credentials

Tyler's institutional career — $3 billion in sales, clients were financial advisors and stockbrokers. Today: FINRA arbitrator. Jason: Certified Exit Planning Advisor (in training). The authority is earned, not claimed.

Chapter 4

The Model

Seven years building ownership models. First vehicles, then real estate, now professional practices. Each model taught them something about how to transfer value without destroying it. Exit to Client was born from that journey.

Chapter 5

The Mission

To help every burned-out advisor who's ready to say it out loud. Not because they need saving — but because they've already done the hard part. They built something successful. Now they just need to see the way out.
