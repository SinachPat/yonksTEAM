For CPAs & Financial Advisors

You built a practice that clients trust. Now you need a way out that doesn't betray them.

Whether you want to rebuild the practice around the life you want, or sell it to the people who trust you most — we'll show you how.

The clock is ticking on the old model.

You're watching the AI headlines. You know the writing is on the wall. Embrace it or watch the practice lose value. Meanwhile, the only exits on offer are ugly: sell to a bigger firm that scatters longtime clients (the kind of sale where clients find out from an email address change), or hold on and ride the value down.

You have two paths forward.

Rebuild the Practice Around Your Life

Keep the practice. Use AI (on your terms) to take the drudgery. Rebuild the schedule so the week fits around your life. The practice still runs — but it doesn't run you.
