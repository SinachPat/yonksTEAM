The Flagship Model

Exit to Client. The sale that serves everyone.

Your clients trust you. They already know what your practice is worth. Why not sell it to them?

Traditional exits leave everyone worse off.

Traditional Exit

  • Sold to the highest bidder
  • Clients find out from an email address change
  • Thirty years of relationships scattered
  • A stranger runs the practice
  • You get a check and disappear

Exit to Client

  • Sold to the people who know you best
  • Clients become owners
  • Thirty years of relationships deepened
  • The community runs the practice
  • You get a check and a legacy

How Exit to Client Works

1

Evaluate

We help you assess your practice's true value — not just the revenue multiple, but the client relationships, the trust capital, and the community you've built over decades.

2

Design the Ownership Structure

We build a model that lets your clients buy in — either as a group or through a structured vehicle. They become owners. You become free. The practice stays in the hands of the people who love it.

3

Transition on Your Terms

You set the timeline. You stay as long as you want — or leave as soon as you're ready. The transition happens when it's right for you, your clients, and your legacy.
