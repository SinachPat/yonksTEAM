Here's how it works.

Three steps. Not a program. A path.

1

Say it out loud.

Admit how you really feel about the thing you built — to people who have felt it too. This is the hardest step, and the one you can't do alone.

2

Picture what replaces it.

Get concrete about the life you want next. What does it need to earn? Do you want the practice rebuilt around that life — or do you want to walk away? If you want to walk away, Exit to Client lets you sell to the people who already know exactly what it's worth: your clients.

3

Wake up wanting the day.

Every change we help you make points at that one outcome. Not a retirement number. Not a valuation multiple. A day you actually want to show up for.

What winning looks like. What losing looks like.

What Winning Looks Like

  • You stop dreading the phone.
  • Your week fits around your life instead of the other way around.
  • Your income stops depending on your calendar.
  • You can answer "what's next" with something you can't wait to start.
  • When you do hand the practice off, it happens on your terms and your timeline.

What Losing Looks Like

  • Keep white-knuckling toward a retirement date that keeps moving.
  • Let the practice keep eating evenings and weekends until health or family makes the decision for you.
  • Sell in a hurry to whoever shows up with a check, and watch thirty years of client relationships get scattered.
  • Keep hating it quietly while everyone keeps congratulating you.
